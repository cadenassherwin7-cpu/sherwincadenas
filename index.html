About

A fieldwork journal, not a security brochure.

This website documents what our group learned while applying information-security and risk-assessment concepts to an organizational setting.

We focus on the reasoning behind assessment: understanding what needs protection, considering impact and probability, recognizing existing controls, selecting reasonable treatment, and reviewing whether safeguards remain useful.
